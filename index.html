shorts-bot

shorts-bot is a private automation tool operated by its owner, Isaiah Theo, to manage his own YouTube channel: checking channel health, reading the channel's own analytics, and (only when the owner enables it) uploading the owner's own original videos.

It is not a public service. No one other than the owner signs in to it, and it does not access anyone else's Google or YouTube account.
